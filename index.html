ABOUT PMR

What is PMR?

Palang Merah Remaja (PMR) is a youth organization that provides students with opportunities to learn, practice useful skills, and participate in humanitarian activities.

Through training and teamwork, PMR members develop responsibility, discipline, leadership, and a spirit of helping others.

"Small actions can make a big difference."
